The Bakery Equation

You are the head pastry chef at "The Infinite Rise." To perfect your signature sourdough, you've discovered that the relationship between ambient temperature ($T$) and fermentation time ($h$) follows a linear path.

"When the kitchen is at 70°F, your bread takes 12 hours. For every degree the temperature rises, the time decreases by exactly 15 minutes."

This isn't just baking—it's a linear function where the crumb structure and flavor profile are directly dictated by the slope of your environment.

Key Concept

Linear Decay

Understanding how independent variables (Temp) inversely affect dependent variables (Time).
